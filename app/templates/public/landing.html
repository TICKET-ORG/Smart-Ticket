How entry works

Three checks before the gate opens.

01

Enroll once

Choose an event, then capture nine guided face samples. Descriptors are extracted on the server — never in the browser.

02

Scan the pass

At the gate, the operator scans your QR. A short verification session opens against that exact ticket, not a shared queue.

03

Face, then entry

The live frame is matched against your enrolled features. A valid match consumes the pass in one atomic update.

Stage 1 · QR token (SHA-256) Stage 2 · Biometric match (HOG + K-NN) On failure · ticket stays active

Under the hood

A classical pipeline you can inspect.

Not a black-box cloud API. Faces are normalized, described with histogram-of-oriented-gradients, and classified with a distance-based K-NN model tuned by particle swarm optimization.

Methodology follows the JESSI 2026 reference on HOG, K-NN, and PSO. Rejection thresholds are calibrated per deployment, not copied from the paper.

Biometrics, handled carefully

The face check happens on the server.

The browser captures a frame; the pipeline runs behind the API. Enrolled features are labeled, stored privately, and used only to verify the ticket in front of the operator.
